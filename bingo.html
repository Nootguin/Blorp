Bingo World Championships

Bingo is more than just a game on Blorp. The Bingo World Championships is the most prestigous and anticipated event in the Blorp calender year for not only Bingo fans but the entire planet. Players compete in various smaller events throughout the season and the Top 100 players with the most points are invited to compete in the Bingo World Championships.

Badminton

This Human invention is not only the planet's fastest growing sport but also the fastest racket sport known in the galaxy, demanding blistering reflexes, explosive power, and chess-like strategy. Played on a rectangular court divided by a high net, the game centers on the shuttlecock (or "blorpie") a unique projectile that behaves unlike any other ball in sports.
